The promises every Scout makes

The Scout Oath: my promise

On my honor I will do my best
to do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law;
to help other people at all times;
to keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.
Duty to God & Country Duty to Others Duty to Self

Show It!

Cub Scout Sign

Right arm straight up, two fingers in a V.

Use it: for the Oath and Law, and to get quiet.

Cub Scout Salute

Two fingers together, touching your right eyebrow.

Use it: to salute the flag and leaders.

Cub Scout Handshake

  1. Shake right hands.
  2. Point your first two fingers straight along the other person's wrist.
  3. Look them in the eye and smile!

Practice with a buddy!

Use it: to greet other Scouts.

The Denner

A den member who helps lead. Everyone gets a turn!

  • Helps set up and clean up
  • Leads the Oath or flag ceremony
  • Helps lead games
  • Sets a good example
