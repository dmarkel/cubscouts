# Bobcat — Webelos Required Adventure (Character & Leadership)

Bobcat is the first required adventure for the Webelos rank. Plan on two den
meetings (requirements 1–6) plus one at-home activity for families (requirement 7).

| File | Who it's for | Print |
|---|---|---|
| [Leader-Guide.pdf](Leader-Guide.pdf) | Den leader: goals, meeting plans, requirement-by-requirement "say it like this" scripts, activities, den tracking sheet, denner rotation, plus printable Human Bingo and Scout Law cards | 1 copy (13 pages; print Human Bingo once per scout) |
| [Scout-Handout.pdf](Scout-Handout.pdf) | Scouts: front has the Scout Oath (with the 3 duties), Scout Law, and motto; back shows the sign, salute, and handshake, the denner's job, a space for the den's Code of Conduct, and a requirement tracker | 1 double-sided sheet per scout |
| [Parent-Letter.pdf](Parent-Letter.pdf) | Families: explains requirement 7 (the at-home *Parent's Guide* activities), with a tear-off slip to confirm completion | 1 per family |

## Requirements covered

1. Get to know members of your den.
2. Recite the Scout Oath and the Scout Law with your den and den leader. Describe 3 points of the Scout Oath.
3. Learn about the Scout Law.
4. Learn about the denner position and responsibilities.
5. With your den, create a den Code of Conduct.
6. Demonstrate the Cub Scout sign, Cub Scout salute, and Cub Scout handshake. Show how each is used.
7. At home, with your parent or legal guardian, do the activities in the booklet *How to Protect Your Children from Child Abuse: A Parent's Guide*.

> Always check requirement wording against the current Webelos Handbook.
