Reach or Throw — Don't Go!

The order of rescue goes from safest to most dangerous for the rescuer.

1REACH

Lie down on the deck and reach out with a pole, pool noodle, towel, or your arm. Stay low so you don't get pulled in!

2THROW

Too far to reach? Throw something that floats. Hold the rope end, yell “Rope!”, and throw it past them.

3ROW

A trained adult uses a boat. Adults only.

4GO

A trained lifeguard swims out with a float. Lifeguards only.

YELL for help → REACH or THROW → DON'T GO!

Beat the Chill!

Hypothermia (hi-po-THER-mee-uh) is when your body loses heat faster than it can make it.
Water steals heat about 25 times faster than air — even on a sunny day!

Warning Sign #1: SHIVERING

Shivering is your body's alarm bell. When you shiver, get out, dry off, and warm up.

If it gets worse, watch for the UMBLES

🦶Stumblestripping, clumsy
🗣️Mumblescan't talk clearly
✋Fumblesfingers don't work
😠Grumblescranky, confused

Also look for blue lips and blue fingers. People who are too cold often don't notice — that's why buddies watch each other!

🛡️ Prevent it

  • Get out when you start shivering
  • Towel off right away
  • Put on dry clothes or a sweatshirt
  • Stay out of the wind
  • Eat a snack and drink water

🚑 Treat it

  • Tell an adult right away!
  • Get out of the water and wind
  • Swap wet clothes for dry ones
  • Wrap up in a blanket or towel
  • Warm (not hot) drink if they're awake

If someone is stumbling, confused, or super sleepy, it's an emergency. Get an adult to call 911.

Swim Day Skills

Remember: requirements that say “attempt” mean try your best. Trying earns it!

Treading Water: stay up, stay calm

  1. Stay tall like you're standing up, chin at the water.
  2. Arms: “spread the peanut butter.” Hands flat, sweep them out and in just under the water.
  3. Legs: pick one. Bicycle (pedal slowly), scissor (one forward, one back), or frog kick.
  4. Go slow and breathe. Splashing tires you out. Relaxing keeps you up!

My goal: seconds    I did: seconds

Swimmer Test

  1. Jump in feet first where it's over your head.
  2. Swim 75 yards with strong strokes (crawl, breaststroke, sidestroke, or trudgen).
  3. Swim 25 yards of easy backstroke.
  4. No stopping! Make at least one sharp turn.
  5. Finish by floating to rest.

Beginner Test

  1. Jump in feet first where it's over your head.
  2. Level off and swim 25 feet.
  3. Stop, turn around sharply.
  4. Swim back to where you started.

Not a beginner or swimmer yet? You're a nonswimmer for now — and every swimmer started there!
