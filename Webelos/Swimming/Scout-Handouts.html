Be a Water-Smart Aquanaut!

Water is fun when we're smart about it.

1. Never Swim Alone

Always have a buddy. When you hear “Buddy check!”:

  1. Grab your buddy's hand
  2. Raise your hands up high
  3. Freeze and be quiet

2. Swim Safe Rules

  • A trained adult and lifeguard are there
  • Tell an adult if you feel sick
  • Stay in your swim area
  • Walk, don't run
  • Listen to the lifeguard

3. Someone in Trouble in the Water?

YELL for help → REACH or THROW → DON'T GO in!

REACH: Lie down and hold out a pool noodle or towel.

THROW: Toss something that floats past them.

4. Too Cold?

Shivering means get out, dry off, and warm up.

Watch your buddy for the Umbles:

Stumbles · Mumbles
Fumbles · Grumbles

See the Umbles? Tell an adult!

5. Swim Day

Tread water: Stay tall, move your arms and legs slowly, and breathe.

Swim test: Try your best. Trying counts!
