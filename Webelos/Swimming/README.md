# Aquanaut — Webelos Swimming Adventure

Aquanaut is the Webelos elective swimming adventure. Plan on two meetings: a
dry-land den meeting (requirements 1–3) and a swim day at a pool with lifeguards
(requirements 4–6).

## Requirements covered

1. State the safety precautions you need to take before doing any swimming activity.
2. Explain the meaning of "order of rescue" and demonstrate the reach and throw rescue techniques from land.
3. Learn how to prevent and treat hypothermia.
4. Attempt to tread water.
5. Attempt the BSA swimmer test.
6. Have 30 minutes or more of free swim time where you practice the buddy system and stay within your ability group. Qualified adult supervision should conduct at least three buddy checks per half hour.

A scout may also earn Aquanaut by passing the BSA swimmer test or by taking
swimming lessons from a qualified instructor.

> Always check requirement wording against the current Webelos Handbook, and run
> every swim under Safe Swim Defense with an adult who has current training.
