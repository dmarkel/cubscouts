Aquanaut Swim Day is coming!

Dear Webelos families,

Our den is working on Aquanaut, the Webelos swimming adventure. In our den meeting, scouts are learning Scouting's swim safety rules (Safe Swim Defense), how to help someone in the water safely from land (Reach or Throw — Don't Go!), and how to prevent and treat hypothermia. Next, we'll put it into practice at the pool.

What happens on swim day

  • Scouts attempt the Scouting America swimmer test, practice treading water, and enjoy 30+ minutes of free swim using the buddy system.
  • The swim test places each scout in an ability group (nonswimmer, beginner, or swimmer) so they swim in water that matches their skills.
  • The requirements say "attempt." Scouts do not need to pass the test to earn the adventure — they just need to give it a try. Nervous swimmers are welcome and will be supported.
  • Trained lifeguards will be on duty, and our swim is run under Safe Swim Defense by an adult with current training.

What to bring

  • Swimsuit (wear it under clothes if possible)
  • Towel
  • Dry sweatshirt or warm layer
  • Goggles (optional)
  • Water bottle and a snack
  • Flip-flops or sandals
  • Plastic bag for wet stuff
  • Sunscreen (if outdoors)

Before swim day, please:

  • Turn in a current Annual Health and Medical Record (Parts A & B) for your scout, and for any adult planning to swim.
  • Let the den leader know about any health concerns, and keep your scout home if they are sick.
  • Talk with your scout about how they feel about swimming — excited, nervous, or somewhere in between is all normal!

Already a swimmer?

A scout can also earn Aquanaut by passing the BSA swimmer test (e.g., at summer camp) or by completing swim lessons with a qualified instructor. If that's your scout, let us know — and please still come join the fun!

Dinner-table quiz — ask your scout: "What does 'Quick Pups Swim Rapidly, Lazy Alligators Bask Daily' stand for?" and "If someone is struggling in the water, what should you do?" (Answer: yell for help, then Reach or Throw — Don't Go!)
